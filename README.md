## 🌟 Overview
**VILLA ATELIER** is a real-time, **computed 3D Three.js architectural walkthrough** of an ultra-luxury \$34.5M modern concrete & glass mansion.

Unlike static 2D image cross-fading, this engine renders an **actual physical 3D architectural model** in WebGL. As you scroll down the page, the 3D camera physically flies forward along a continuous **3D Catmull-Rom spline curve** through the residence:

$$\text{Driveway \& Reflection Pool} \longrightarrow \text{Stone Bridge} \longrightarrow \text{12-ft Bronze Pivot Door} \longrightarrow \text{Double-Height Foyer \& Curved Stair} \longrightarrow \text{Great Living Salon} \longrightarrow \text{Infinity Pool Terrace} \longrightarrow \text{Upper Master Sky Sanctuary}$$

---

## 🏗️ 3D Architectural Spaces Modeled in Real-Time WebGL
1. **01 / Approach & Reflection Pool**: Pre-stressed concrete cantilevers floating over shimmering 3D reflection waters.
2. **02 / The Pivot Threshold**: An architectural stone bridge crossing directly to an illuminated 12-ft bronze pivot door (modeled ajar to invite the camera inside).
3. **03 / The Grand Travertine Foyer**: Stepping inside onto Italian travertine slabs under a curved bronze staircase with concealed LED step illumination.
4. **04 / The Great Salon & Hearth**: A 24-ft double-height living room featuring an L-shaped modular sectional sofa, marble coffee table, motorized sliding glass walls, and a floating linear hearth with **animated flickering flame particles**.
5. **05 / The Horizon Infinity Terrace**: Stepping out onto the rear stone terrace to a 65-ft zero-edge pool with real-time shimmering water, floating volcanic stone fire bowl, and sunken outdoor lounge.
6. **06 / The Master Sky Sanctuary**: Ascending into the cantilevered upper floor featuring fluted white oak walls, platform king bed, and corner frameless glass walls.

## 🗺️ Interactive Architectural House Map
- Positioned in a sleek floating glass card at the bottom right.
- Renders the floorplan walking path with 6 interactive waypoint nodes (`Approach`, `Pivot Door`, `Foyer`, `Great Salon`, `Pool Deck`, `Master Suite`).
- **Click any node** to smoothly glide the 3D camera directly into that space along the spline curve!
- An animated cyan drone traveler pin tracks your exact live position as you scroll.

## ⌨️ Filming Hotkeys (Hands-Free Video Recording)
| Key | Action | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Space` or `R`** | **Automated Walkthrough** | Initiates an automated, buttery smooth 28-second continuous 3D camera flight along the entire spline path. Record hands-free! |
| **`L`** | **Lighting Toggle** | Switches between **Midnight Obsidian** (deep blue hour, glowing warm LEDs, flickering fire) and **Golden Hour** (warm amber sunset). |
| **`M`** | **Web Audio Sound** | Toggles procedural luxury ambient drone synthesizer & interactive micro-click audio. |
| **Mouse / Trackpad** | **Interactive Parallax** | Move your mouse or trackpad at any point to subtly look around in 3D while the camera travels! |
